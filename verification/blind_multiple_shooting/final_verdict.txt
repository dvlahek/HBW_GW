BLIND QNM VERIFICATION - FINAL VERDICT
======================================

1. Schwarzschild benchmark
   PASS at the production resolution Rmax=40. Relative complex error for the
   l=2 fundamental Zerilli QNM is 1.19e-5, below the 1e-4 gate.

2. Stable candidates in the blind matter search window
   ONE.

3. Complex frequency
   M omega = 0.3775148115 - 0.0208716540 i

   Nine seed/lambda-pair combinations converged to the same neighbourhood.
   All attempted seeds, including failures, are reported in
   all_frequency_seeds.csv.

4. Outer-boundary stability
   |D| = 8.9e-7 at Rmax=25,
         6.4e-8 at Rmax=40,
         9.2e-8 at Rmax=60.

5. Full 7x4 bulk SVD residuals
   7.3e-15 and 5.8e-15 for the two physical radial roots.
   Their sum is -0.222222222... = -2/9, although this relation was not
   imposed in the blind derivation.

6. Was the target matter-QNM frequency supplied before the search?
   NO.

7. Numerical method
   Real-axis Riccati multiple shooting for both vacuum regions with
   scipy.integrate.solve_ivp. No exterior complex scaling and no Chebyshev
   boundary-value solver are used. The Einstein--Vlasov bulk matrix is
   separately derived symbolically, and chi_J(omega), chi_X(omega) are
   evaluated from the supplied spectral nodes at every trial frequency.

Additional audit
   A 24-point argument-principle contour gives winding number 1.000000.

The verification was performed within the PiPi institutional research project
at the Faculty of Organization and Informatics. Anthropic Claude was used as
a coding assistant during implementation. The researchers defined the blind
protocol and validation criteria and inspected the code and audit outputs
before unblinding.
